Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026 · Published by Vorbitlabs · Android app com.escapein10s.game

In short

  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.
  • You don't create an account. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.
  • The app has no analytics, no tracking and, in this version, no ads.

Who we are

Escape in 10s is a puzzle game made by Vorbitlabs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Escape in 10s app on Android.

Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following in the app's private storage on your phone. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

WhatWhy
Rooms cleared, level, XP, stars, hearts, best escape time and streakSo you can continue where you left off
The player name you choose and a random player ID (for example #ESC-12345)Shown on your profile screen
Daily challenge resultsTo track your daily streak
Settings: sound, haptic feedback, reduced motionTo keep your preferences

The player name is whatever you type; you don't need to use your real name. To delete all of this data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings (Settings → Apps → Escape in 10s → Storage → Clear data).

Information we collect

None. The app does not send your game data, name, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information to us or to anyone else. There is no sign-in and no server that receives your data.

Content loaded from Google

When it runs, the app downloads its text fonts and icons from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) and a few cover images from Google's image servers (lh3.googleusercontent.com). Like any download, these requests share standard technical information with Google, such as your IP address and basic device and browser details. We do not receive this information. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing and copying

The profile screen has a Share button and a Copy button for your player ID. Share opens your phone's own share menu with a short message about your score; nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself. Copy puts your player ID on your clipboard. We don't see either.

Permissions

The app only requests internet access, which it uses to load the fonts and images described above. If haptic feedback is turned on, the app may vibrate your phone during play. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Advertising

This version of Escape in 10s shows no ads. We plan to add ads from Google AdMob in a future update. Ad services can collect information such as your device's advertising ID and how you interact with ads. Before any update with ads is released, we will update this policy to explain exactly what is collected, how you can control it, and how to opt out of personalized ads.

Children

Escape in 10s does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has somehow shared personal information with us, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes, such as adding ads, will be reflected here before the app update that introduces them.
